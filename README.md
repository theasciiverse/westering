# Westering

**TWO THOUSAND MILES. FIVE OF YOU. ONE WAGON.**

Independence, Missouri, spring 1848. Between you and the Willamette Valley
lie the prairie, the Platte, the Rockies, the Snake River desert and the
Blue Mountains. Name your party, choose the month you leave, and spend your
$700 at the store: oxen, provisions, clothing, powder and shot, and spare
parts for a wagon that will break.

Then the road. Set the pace and the rations, rest when someone is failing,
hunt buffalo on the plains and elk in the mountains, trade with the trains
you meet. Ford, float or pay the ferry at seven rivers, choose the Sublette
Cutoff or Fort Bridger, the Barlow Road or the Columbia's rapids. Sickness,
broken axles, strayed oxen, hail and bad water come as they came on the
real trail, and every grave you leave behind gets the words you give it.

Twenty-five landmarks of the 1848 road, from Chimney Rock and Independence
Rock to South Pass and Fort Hall, each with a note on what stood there that
year.

## Play

With [av](https://asciiverse.sh), the Asciiverse client:

```
av install westering
av play westering
```

## Controls

Arrow keys to choose, Enter to do it, Esc to go back. On the trail, at
landmarks and at rivers the number keys pick at once. Esc on the trail
asks whether to stop for now (`y` stops, `n` stays): the journey is kept,
and `c` on the title page takes it up again. In the store, Right or `+`
buys and Left or `-` puts back.
